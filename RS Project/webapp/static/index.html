How it works

Two recommenders, side by side.
CB

Content‑Based

Compares movies by their genres, cast, directors, writers, and tagline using TF‑IDF similarity. We surface the overlapping terms so you see exactly why a recommendation matches.

CF

Collaborative

Builds an item‑item similarity matrix from a simulated audience of taste profiles. Picks for you are titles that "co‑rate" similarly to the seed.

XAI

Explainable

Every card answers the question "why this one?" — shared terms for content, ratings affinity for collaborative. No black boxes.
